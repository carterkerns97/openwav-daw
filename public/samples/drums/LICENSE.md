# Openwave drum sample license

The drum one-shots in the `808`, `909`, and `505` folders were sourced
from the open-source [0x808](https://github.com/averagenative/0x808)
project's bundled sample library.

The upstream sample manifest identifies these folders as royalty-free,
with no attribution required. They are redistributed here for use in the
Openwave Drum Rack. The samples are not affiliated with or endorsed by
the original hardware manufacturers.
